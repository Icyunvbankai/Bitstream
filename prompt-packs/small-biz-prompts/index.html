How to use this pack (60 seconds)

  1. Open a free AI tool — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com), or Meta AI. No paid plan needed.
  2. Copy any prompt and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your business details.
  3. Feed it your real numbers. Your costs, your prices, your customer complaints — specifics in, quality out. "Help my marketing" gets fluff. "I run a mobile car detailing business in Fort Pierce, average ticket $85, most customers come from referrals" gets a plan.
  4. Treat the first answer as a draft. Reply with "make it shorter," "more direct," or "rewrite for a customer who knows nothing about this" until it's right.
  5. Save the winners. When a prompt gives you something great — a script, a policy, a post — save the output in a notes app. You're building your business playbook.
Rule 1 — Describe, don't write. You don't need marketing skills or business-school language. Describe what you want in plain words — "a friendly sign for the door explaining our new hours" — and the AI does the writing.
Rule 2 — Verify money and legal. For pricing, taxes, contracts, or employment answers, treat AI output as a strong first draft, not gospel. Check it against your accountant, your state rules, or a quick search before you act.
Rule 3 — One system at a time. Don't try to AI your whole business today. Pick the fire that's burning — the slow week, the employee problem, the pricing that's too low — and fix that first.

1 · Marketing & Content That Converts

Get found, get followed, get customers — marketing that sounds like you, not a corporate brochure.

Stop guessing at prices and hoping there's profit left — know your numbers cold.

PROMPT 40

Honest about [rising costs / added value], appreciative of their loyalty, and confident — no groveling. Include a line for existing customers [grandfathered rate / loyalty thank-you].
PROMPT 42
