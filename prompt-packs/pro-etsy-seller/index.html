How to use this pack (60 seconds)

  1. Open a free AI tool — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), or Gemini (gemini.google.com). No paid plan needed.
  2. Copy any prompt and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your shop details.
  3. Be specific. Your product, your prices, your buyer — specifics in, quality out. "Write a description" gets generic. "Write a description for my $42 personalized walnut cutting board for wedding gifts" gets a listing.
  4. Treat the first answer as a draft. Reply with "make it shorter," "more gift-focused," or "add keywords" until it is right.
  5. Paste the winners into Etsy. These prompts are built for Etsy fields: 140-character titles, 13 tags, descriptions, policies, and messages.
Rule 1 — One listing at a time. Do not try to SEO your whole shop today. Pick your bestseller, run the SEO prompts on it, then move to the next.
Rule 2 — Verify money and policy answers. AI can draft pricing math and policy language, but double-check Etsy fees, shipping rates, and legal disclaimers yourself before publishing.
Rule 3 — Your voice wins. If a draft does not sound like you, say so: "rewrite in my voice — warm, a little funny, no corporate speak." Buyers buy from people.
