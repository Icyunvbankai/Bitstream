How to use this pack (60 seconds)

  1. Open a free AI tool — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com), or Meta AI. No account tricks, no paid plan needed.
  2. Copy any prompt and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your details.
  3. Be specific. Your kids' ages, your budget, your constraints — specifics in, quality out. "Plan a trip" gets a generic list. "Plan a Saturday adventure for a 7-year-old near Fort Pierce under $40" gets a plan.
  4. Treat the first answer as a draft. Reply with "make it shorter," "more casual," or "add prices" until it's right.
  5. Talk instead of typing. Every one of these tools has a voice mode — ramble while you walk, let it turn the ramble into clean text.
Rule 1 — Describe, don't write. You don't need to write well. You just need to describe what you want in plain words. The AI does the writing.
Rule 2 — Verify money and facts. For prices, medical, or legal answers, ask for sources — or use Perplexity, which cites where everything came from so you can check it yourself.
Rule 3 — One dad-win at a time. Don't try to AI your whole fatherhood today. Pick one prompt. The science project, the brisket, the budget talk you've been putting off.
