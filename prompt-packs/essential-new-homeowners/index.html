How to use this pack (60 seconds)

  1. Open a free AI tool — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), or Gemini (gemini.google.com). No account tricks, no paid plan needed.
  2. Copy any prompt and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your details.
  3. Be specific. Your house's age, size, location, and budget — specifics in, quality out. "My AC is broken" gets generic advice. "My 15-year-old central AC in Florida just died, I have $4,000, and it's July" gets a real plan.
  4. Treat the first answer as a draft. Reply with "give me the cheapest safe option," "what can I do this weekend," or "explain it like I've never owned a home" until it's right.
  5. AI plans, a licensed pro signs off. These prompts are great for checklists, estimates, questions to ask, and learning the lingo. For electrical, plumbing, gas, structural, or mold issues, always have a licensed professional verify before work begins.
Rule 1 — Never let AI approve dangerous work. If a prompt involves electrical panels, gas lines, load-bearing walls, roofs, or sewage, use the answer as a starting checklist and get a licensed pro's sign-off. AI doesn't carry insurance — your contractor should.
Rule 2 — Get 3 quotes, always. AI can help you price a job and write the questions to ask — but the quotes come from real local contractors. No AI answer replaces a site visit.
Rule 3 — Small house wins first. Don't try to AI your whole renovation in one weekend. Pick the leak, the breaker, or the one room that's driving you crazy.
