How to use this pack (60 seconds)

  1. Open a free AI tool — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com), or Meta AI. No paid plan needed.
  2. Copy any prompt and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your listing, client, or market details.
  3. Be specific. Bed/bath count, neighborhood, price, client type — specifics in, quality out. "Write a listing description" gets generic copy. "Write a 200-word listing description for a 4-bed, 2.5-bath pool home in Fort Pierce under $425k" gets copy that sells.
  4. Treat the first answer as a draft. Reply with "make it warmer," "add urgency," or "cut it to 150 words" until it's right.
  5. Save your winners. When a prompt produces copy you love, paste it back in and say "use this tone for all my listing descriptions."
Rule 1 — AI drafts, your broker verifies. Anything touching contracts, disclosures, pricing strategy, or fair housing: let the AI organize and draft, then verify with your broker and legal counsel before it reaches a client. Never let AI give legal conclusions or definitive contract advice.
Rule 2 — Describe the house, not the people. Keep every listing, ad, and neighborhood description fair-housing safe: talk about the property, the amenities, and the facts — never who should live there. When in doubt, run prompt 025.
Rule 3 — Protect client data. Strip names, addresses, and financial details before pasting anything into a free AI tool. Use "[Client]" and "[address]" — specifics about the house, not the humans.
