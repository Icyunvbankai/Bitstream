How to use this pack (60 seconds)

  1. Open a free AI tool — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com), or Meta AI. No paid plan needed.
  2. Copy any prompt and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your details.
  3. Be specific. Your kids' ages, your budget, your dietary needs — specifics in, quality out. "Plan meals" gets a generic list. "Plan 5 weeknight dinners for a family of 5 with one vegetarian and a nut allergy, under $150" gets a plan.
  4. Treat the first answer as a draft. Reply with "make it shorter," "simpler for a 6-year-old," or "cheaper" until it's right.
  5. Talk instead of typing. Every one of these tools has a voice mode — ramble while you fold laundry, let it turn the ramble into clean text.
Rule 1 — Describe, don't write. You don't need to write well. You just need to describe what you want in plain words. The AI does the writing.
Rule 2 — Verify money, medical, and legal. For prices, health, or legal answers, ask for sources — or use Perplexity, which cites where everything came from so you can check it yourself.
Rule 3 — One win at a time. Don't try to AI your whole life today. Pick one prompt. The dinner plan, the chore chart, the hard conversation you've been putting off.
