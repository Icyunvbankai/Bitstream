How to use this pack (60 seconds)

  1. Open a free AI tool — ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com), or Meta AI. No paid plan needed for any of these prompts.
  2. Copy any prompt and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your details — your major, your class, your deadline.
  3. Be specific. "Help me study" gets generic advice. "Quiz me on organic chemistry mechanisms for Friday's midterm, 15 questions, hardest last" gets a study session.
  4. Treat the first answer as a draft. Reply with "quiz me harder," "explain that part again," or "make it a checklist" until it's right.
  5. Use it to learn, not to cheat. These prompts are built for understanding — outlines, quizzes, feedback, explanations. For graded work, check your school's AI policy first.
Rule 1 — Describe, don't write. You don't need to prompt like an expert. Just describe your situation in plain words — the class, the deadline, what's confusing you. The AI does the heavy lifting.
Rule 2 — Verify everything that matters. For citations, statistics, financial aid details, or anything going into a graded paper, double-check against real sources. AI is confident even when it's wrong.
Rule 3 — One win at a time. Don't try to AI your whole semester tonight. Pick one prompt — the exam Friday, the paper outline, the budget that's bleeding. Start there.

1 · Study Smarter, Not Harder

Your 24/7 tutor with infinite patience — for midterms, finals, and everything in between.

PROMPT 01

The "explain it like I'm five" reset

Explain [photosynthesis / supply and demand / the Krebs cycle] like I'm five years old, using one everyday analogy. Then level the explanation up to college level once I say "got it."
PROMPT 02

Socratic tutor mode

Don't just give me the answer. Teach me [organic chemistry reaction mechanisms] Socratically — ask me guiding questions, wait for my answer, and only correct me when I'm wrong. Here's what I already know: [describe it].
PROMPT 03

Quiz me before the exam

Quiz me on [the causes of the French Revolution] for my [history midterm]. Ask one question at a time, tell me if I'm right or wrong, explain what I missed, then move on. 15 questions, hardest ones last.
PROMPT 04

Flashcard factory

Turn these lecture notes into 25 flashcards — term on the front, definition under 25 words on the back. Bold the terms most likely to be tested: [paste notes].
PROMPT 05

One-page study guide

Condense these [40 pages of psych notes] into a one-page study guide: only the highest-yield concepts, one concrete example each, in exam-day order: [paste notes].
PROMPT 06

Analogy that makes it click

I don't get [how TCP/IP works]. Explain it with an analogy to [something I know: ordering food delivery / group chats]. Then test me with 3 quick questions to check it stuck.
PROMPT 07

Step-by-step math tutor

Walk me through [integration by parts] one step at a time. Give me one worked example, then one problem for ME to solve. Don't move on until I get it right.
PROMPT 08

Concept map builder

Map how these concepts connect for my [biology final]: [list 8-10 terms]. Show me the hierarchy — which are the big ideas, which are details underneath, and where students usually mix them up.
PROMPT 09

Predict the exam

Based on these lecture slides and what my professor emphasized, predict the 10 most likely questions for my [econ 201 final] — and tell me WHY each one is likely: [paste slides or describe emphasis].
PROMPT 10

Memory palace walkthrough

Help me memorize [the 12 cranial nerves in order] using a memory palace in my [dorm room]. Walk me through placing each item, then quiz me on the walk.
PROMPT 11

Wrong-answer autopsy

I got this practice problem wrong: [paste the problem and my answer]. Don't just give me the right answer — diagnose exactly where my thinking went off track and which concept I misunderstood.
PROMPT 12

Feynman technique check

I'm going to explain [natural selection] back to you in my own words. Listen for gaps, fuzzy logic, and jargon I'm hiding behind — then tell me what to fix: [my explanation].
PROMPT 13

The 24-hour cram plan

My [chemistry] final is in [24 hours] and I know about [40%] of the material. Build me an hour-by-hour plan: what to study, when to quiz myself, when to sleep. Be ruthless about priorities.

Outlines, feedback, and revision help — everything except doing the work for you.

PROMPT 17

PROMPT 18

Counterargument generator

PROMPT 19

Paragraph structure check

PROMPT 20

Transition smoother

PROMPT 21

Cut the fluff

PROMPT 22

Citation-needed spotter

PROMPT 23

Academic tone shift

PROMPT 24

Title brainstorm

Give me 10 title options for my paper on [topic] with this thesis: [thesis]. Mix clever and straightforward — and tell me which one a professor would actually respect.
PROMPT 25

Peer-review mode

Act as a tough-but-fair peer reviewer for my [1,200-word] draft. Score it on thesis, evidence, structure, and clarity — then give me the 5 highest-impact fixes in priority order: [paste draft].
