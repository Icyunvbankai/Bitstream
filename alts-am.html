Edition #002

The Week in Alts: the liquidity trade

Saturday, October 3, 2026

THE LEAD

Private equity's newest product is itself. Blue Owl is nearing the close of a $2.7 billion secondary transaction on its Dyal Capital Partners IV flagship — structured as a fund-backed note that moves a slice of its minority stakes in private asset managers into a new vehicle with $1 billion of equity plus $1.7 billion of debt raised against the underlying assets (Bloomberg Law). Meanwhile Clearlake wrapped a $1 billion collateralized fund obligation — pledging $600M of its private credit exposure, $200M of a PE secondaries fund, and another $200M PE stake to sell $775M of bonds and $225M of equity (Bloomberg). GPs aren't waiting for exits anymore; they're securitizing their own balance sheets. In a year when H1 secondary volume ran at a record pace, the exit has become a product line.

Sources: Bloomberg Law; Bloomberg via AltGoesMainstream (Oct 2)

FUND CLOSES

  • Investcorp — $1.22B for its second North American private equity fund, beating its $1.1B target, targeting growing middle-market services businesses. (AltAssets, Oct 2)
  • Headline — $400M eighth European early-stage fund, doubling down on AI (backed Mistral, Black Forest Labs). (AltAssets, Oct 2)
  • The record week that was: Ares $4.2B (4x target), Audax Private Debt $10B, Oaktree $2B asset-backed finance, Carlyle $2.3B infra credit, Pinegrove $1.5B, Stride $550M, Princeton Equity $1.3B, DIG Ventures $120M, SWEN €236M — all closed in the last five days. (AltAssets; covered in full in Edition #001)

DEALS

  • SoftBank completed its $3.1B acquisition of DigitalBridge, taking the digital infrastructure specialist private as a controlled subsidiary. (AltAssets, Oct 2)
  • Wendel sealed a €2.1B exit of Stahl. (AltAssets, Oct 2)
  • Blackstone is investing $1B to form Falcata, a US defense technology company specializing in missile and drone interceptors, with plans to bolt on more companies. The firm has invested $20B in defense and aerospace over the past five years. (Bloomberg via AltGoesMainstream, Oct 2)
  • Harrison Street agreed to acquire a majority stake in Vicinity Energy at a $2.92B valuation from Antin Infrastructure Partners. (AltAssets)

REGULATION & POLICY

  • The SEC voted September 30 to propose rules expanding retail access to private markets: a framework letting advisers to regulated funds take performance-based compensation of up to 20% of net gains, a modernized interval fund rule, and multi-share-class issuance for closed-end funds — plus a comment request on letting accredited status come from professional credentials. Chairman Atkins tied it to Trump's Executive Order on democratizing alt access for 401(k)s. (securities.io, citing SEC release)
  • The cautionary counterpoint: Australia's Metrics Credit Partners (A$40B) suspended trading in three ASX-listed funds after auditor KPMG disagreed with valuation assumptions — net tangible asset values cut up to 12.16%, with "greater weight given to downside scenarios." Australia's corporate regulator has warned of unrealistic valuations and poor governance across the country's booming private credit sector. (Reuters, Sep 28)

ALLOCATOR INTEL

  • Colorado FPPA deployed $491M across alternatives: $336M to six new manager relationships, $139M to four existing firms, $15.8M in co-invests. The largest ticket: $150M to Aldine Capital Fund V (subordinated debt + equity for lower-middle-market companies). (Connect Money, Oct 2)
  • Singapore's GIC is testing the market with a ~$1B PE secondary sale of stakes in ~30 funds (Blackstone, Apollo, TDR; average vintage 2016), Evercore advising — routine portfolio management from a fund that remains a buyer too. (PE Insights / Bloomberg)
  • Korea Investment Corp is separately exploring a $1B+ PE secondary sale, PJT Partners advising — two of Asia's biggest sovereign funds in the same market at once. (Markets Group)
  • In September, Ardian preemptively acquired a GIC private credit portfolio at a premium to book value — bids are that competitive for quality paper. (Markets Group)

THE NUMBER

$124B — global secondary transaction volume in H1 2026, up roughly 28% year-on-year ($63B LP-led, $61B GP-led), per Markets Group's Institutional Allocator Outlook. The liquidity market is now big enough to absorb a sovereign wealth fund's portfolio twice over.

MEME CLEANSER

Clearlake raised $1 billion by collateralizing its own funds. Blue Owl raised $2.7 billion against stakes in other managers' funds. The industry that charges 2-and-20 to deploy capital has discovered the ultimate fee arbitrage: deploying capital against itself.

Edition #001

The Week in Alts (launch edition)

Saturday, October 3, 2026

THE LEAD

Private credit just had its loudest week of the year. Ares Management raised $4.2 billion for its debut structured solutions strategy — more than 4x the vehicle's original $1 billion target — on demand for financing aimed directly at private equity managers. Two days earlier, Audax Private Debt stacked $10 billion for direct lending, the largest pool of capital in its nearly three-decade history ($5.4B in equity commitments plus leverage and SMAs). And Oaktree closed its first asset-backed finance fund at $2 billion, with parent Brookfield's wider ABF platform now past $60 billion. Three data points, one story: the bid for private credit paper is not slowing down.

Sources: AltAssets (Oct 2), WSJ Pro Private Equity (Sep 30), Alternative Credit Investor (Oct 1)

FUND CLOSES

  • Ares — $4.2B debut structured solutions raise, 4x target. (AltAssets)
  • Oaktree (Brookfield) — $2B debut asset-backed finance fund; commitments from US public pensions and sovereign wealth funds. (Alternative Credit Investor)
  • Audax Private Debt — $10B direct-lending strategy; lends to PE-owned middle-market companies at $15–75M EBITDA. (WSJ Pro)
  • Pinegrove — $1.5B oversubscribed Strategic Investors Fund XII (venture managers + co-invests), anchored by Florida State Board of Administration. (Connect Money)
  • Stride Consumer Partners — $550M Fund II, hit the hard cap in under six months (31% bigger than Fund I). (WSJ Pro)
  • Princeton Equity Group — $1.3B for franchise and multi-location businesses, 2x its predecessor. (WSJ Pro)
  • Carlyle — $2.3B close for sophomore infrastructure credit fund, beating target. (Creditflux)
  • DIG Ventures — $120M third fund for Europe's AI infrastructure startups. (AltAssets)
  • SWEN Capital — €236M first close on Article 9 Europa PE fund, 80% already committed. (AltAssets)

DEALS

  • Harrison Street agreed to acquire a majority stake in Vicinity Energy (district energy) at a $2.92B valuation, from Antin Infrastructure Partners. (AltAssets)
  • India's primary market is on fire: companies raised a record $25.27B in H1 FY27, up 75% year-on-year, even as the secondary market slumped — NSE's $2.3B issue and SBI Funds Management's $1.03B IPO led the way. (Reuters)
  • a16z's American Dynamism teamed with Atlas Holdings to launch Foundry Management, embedding technical founders inside industrial portfolio companies. (WSJ Pro)

REGULATION & POLICY

  • The SEC wants to open private markets to retail. The Commission approved three measures making it easier for private-fund managers to raise from ordinary Americans — including changes to the accredited-investor definition and performance-fee rules. A structural demand unlock if it sticks. (WSJ Pro, Oct 1)
  • Apax is set to launch Apax XII at ~€12B (~$13.5B), joining CVC, Permira, and Bridgepoint in a crowded European megafund field. (WSJ Pro, Oct 2)

ALLOCATOR INTEL

  • Florida State Board of Administration anchored Pinegrove's $1.5B SIF XII — "one of our most important and successful partnerships," per its PE head. Florida pensions also backed Stride's $550M close alongside Colorado, Indiana, and New York retirement systems.
  • PitchBook: distressed debt among the 15 largest listed BDCs rose 64% to $6.8B in the year to June 30 — the dislocation bid behind this week's credit raises.

THE NUMBER

$25.27B — record Indian equity fundraising in half a year, up 75%, while the Nifty managed 1.3%. Domestic liquidity is doing the heavy lifting where foreign flows won't.

MEME CLEANSER

Ares targeted $1B and raised $4.2B. Somewhere a placement agent is updating their LinkedIn headline to "4.2x oversubscribed" as we speak.
